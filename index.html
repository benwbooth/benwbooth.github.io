Apps and games

Kog

A music player for everything. Albums, audiobooks, MIDI, tracker modules, and game soundtracks live in one library, even inside archives, with SID, SPC, VGM, and PSF next to MP3 and FLAC. A shared Rust core drives a Qt desktop app with Winamp skins, a terminal player, a web player, and a headless server. Native iPhone and Android apps are in development. Downloads

Earshot

A music player that finds the music for you: charts for every genre and decade, radio stations made of the songs people share on Reddit, real albums and artist pages, and mixes built from what you play. It plays from YouTube, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, Mixcloud, and the Internet Archive in one Rust and Qt app, with a web version for phones. Downloads

Almanac

Mail, calendars, and contacts in one desktop app for Linux, Windows, and macOS. It talks to your accounts directly over IMAP, SMTP, CalDAV, and CardDAV, with easy sign-in for Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Fastmail, and more. It stays fast with hundreds of thousands of messages, writes rich text with Markdown and highlighted code blocks, and answers invitations from the message. Rust with a Qt Quick interface. Downloads

Lunchpail

A game library and emulator frontend for Windows, macOS, and Linux. It handles box art, manuals, video previews, 3D boxes, controller setup, save-state resume, CRT shaders, bezels, translation patches, and RetroAchievements, then launches RetroArch or a standalone emulator. Couch Mode turns it all into a cover wall you can drive from the sofa. Downloads

GameBuddyprototype

An AI game companion you talk to while playing. It builds walkthroughs from guides and videos, watches the screen to track progress, warns about nearby hazards, and overlays maps and next steps.

HeroQuest 3D

The 1989 board game on a virtual table, in native Rust: board, miniatures, cards, physical dice, and a computer game master running the 14 original US quests. An unofficial fan project. Downloads

RetroTube Shaders

A Chrome and Brave extension that runs 3,757 RetroArch presets on YouTube videos in real time, with bezels, ambient light, and a virtual 240p source. Downloads

qbasic-rs

A QBasic clone for the terminal: a simulation of the MS-DOS IDE and its interpreter in Rust, drawn with raw ANSI escapes, down to the dialogs and graphics commands. Downloads

Reverse engineering and game ports

Star Fox HDin progress

Pure Rust reimplementations of Star Fox and Star Fox 2, ported from the SNES assembly, with a native renderer and an SPC-700 audio core.

Commander Blood Toolsin progress

A Rust reimplementation of the Commander Blood DOS engine that runs the original CD data, plus media extraction and script recovery tools. It also runs Big Bug Bang. Downloads

Legacy of the Wizard

A playable native Rust port of the NES game, with its own PPU and APU shims. The ROM stays a runtime input. Downloads

Quik Majik Adventure

A checksum-exact Turbo C reconstruction of the 1991 DOS game, plus a Rust translation extended into a full campaign with restored quests and save/load. Bring your own game files.

Castle Adventure

A Rust port of the 1984 DOS game, decoded from its compiled BASIC and data files, with modding notes. Downloads

altirraqt

A Qt6 port of Avery Lee's Altirra Atari 8-bit emulator for Linux, macOS, and Windows. An LLM did most of the translation under my direction, as its README explains. Downloads
